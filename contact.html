FAQ

Can I submit my own recipe to the blog? ＋

Absolutely! We love featuring community recipes. Use the contact form above and select "Other" as the subject. Include your recipe name and a brief description.

Do you work with food brands and sponsors? ＋

Yes! We're open to brand collaborations that align with our values of quality, sustainability, and real food. Reach out via the form with "Collaboration" selected.

Can I repost or share a recipe from the blog? ＋

You're welcome to share any recipe with credit and a link back to Savor & Soul. Please don't reproduce full recipes without permission.

How often are new recipes published? ＋

We publish 2–3 new recipes every week. Subscribe to our newsletter to never miss a new post!
